Upcoming rides and events

Tuesday Easy Spin

An easy 15 miles at a conversational pace. Great for new riders, kids on trailers, and anyone coming back after a break. We regroup at every turn and finish at the coffee shop on Main Street.

Thursday Hill Repeats

Six climbs, one big hill, no mercy. Bring water.

Saturday Gravel Grinder

Forty-five miles of Driftless gravel with two store stops and a long descent that makes the climbing worth it. Bring a spare tube and a patch kit, because the roads are rough and the farms are far apart.

Sunday Family Roll

Eight flat miles on the paved trail, with snacks halfway through. Helmets for everyone, and a ride leader at the back so nobody gets left behind.

Bike Fit Clinic

Thirty minutes with a certified fitter. Saddle height, reach, cleats, and the small changes that stop knee pain before it starts. Ask about the free first tune-up while you are in.
