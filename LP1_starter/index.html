Come Hungry. Leave Squeaky.

Three days of fried curds, cheese-carving contests, and Wisconsin's biggest dairy celebration — right here at the fairgrounds.

About the Festival

Every August, thousands of visitors gather to celebrate the food Wisconsin is famous for. This year's festival features over 40 vendors, live music, and the return of the Curd Toss competition.
