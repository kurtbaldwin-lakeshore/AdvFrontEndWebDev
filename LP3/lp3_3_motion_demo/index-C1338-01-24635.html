2. The animation zoo (never stop)

Six animations that loop forever. All of them use only transform or opacity.

Spin

rotate 360°, 1s, linear, infinite

Bounce

translateY up and down, 0.8s, infinite

Pulse

scale 1 to 1.25 and back, 1.2s, infinite

Wiggle

rotate -18° to 18°, 0.5s, infinite

Slide

translateX back and forth, 1.5s, alternate

Blink

opacity 1 to 0.15, 1s, infinite
