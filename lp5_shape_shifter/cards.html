How the grid works

.card-grid: display: flex, flex-wrap: wrap, gap: 1.5rem
Cards move to the next row when they run out of room, with even space between them.
.card: flex: 1 0 280px
Grow 1, shrink 0, start at 280px. Cards share spare room but never get narrower than 280px.
.card: max-width: 400px
Stops a lone card from stretching across a wide row.
transition
transform and box-shadow ease over 0.25s, the same idea as LP3.
:hover and :focus-visible
The card lifts 4px and the shadow grows, for mouse and keyboard users.
