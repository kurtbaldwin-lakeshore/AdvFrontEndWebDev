Our story

In the spring of 1987, Walt Hollander cleared a corner of his garage in Viroqua and began fixing neighborhood bikes for a few dollars and a cup of coffee. Today his daughter Pat runs the shop on Main Street. The idea is the same: treat every rider like a neighbor and explain every repair.
