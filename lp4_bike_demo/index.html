Bike Care Guide

Ten minutes of care before a ride saves hours of repair later. Here are the basics every rider should know.

Prefer paper? Print our care checklist (PDF) and tape it to the garage wall.

Tire Pressure

Check your tires before every ride. Too soft and you risk a pinch flat; too hard and the ride gets harsh. The right number is printed on the tire sidewall.

  • Road: 80–100 PSI
  • Gravel: 35–50 PSI
  • E-bike: 40–65 PSI
  • Kids: 20–30 PSI

Chain Care

  1. Wipe the chain with a clean rag.
  2. Add one drop of lube to each link.
  3. Wipe off the extra so dirt does not stick.

Brake Check

Squeeze each lever. The brake should grab the wheel before the lever reaches half of its travel.

If the lever feels spongy or touches the handlebar, stop riding and bring the bike in.

Winter Storage

  • Clean and lube the drivetrain.
  • Store the bike indoors, off the floor if you can.
  • Charge e-bike batteries to about 60% before storing.
