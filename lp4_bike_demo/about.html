Who We Are

We are a team of three mechanics, riders, and neighbors. Since 1987 we have kept the Driftless Region rolling, one flat tire and one first bike at a time.

We believe a good bike shop should feel like a clubhouse: coffee on the counter, no judgment about your questions, and a ride leaving the front door every few days.

Built in the Driftless since 1987

Four hills, two rivers, and more gravel roads than you could ride in a lifetime. This is home.
