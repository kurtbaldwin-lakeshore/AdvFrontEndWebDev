From a Garage to Main Street

In the spring of 1987, Walt Hollander cleared a corner of his garage in Viroqua, hung a hand-painted sign, and began fixing neighborhood bikes for a few dollars and a cup of coffee. Word travelled fast.

Today his daughter Pat runs the shop on Main Street. The tools are newer and the bikes are fancier, but the idea is the same: treat every rider like a neighbor.

Timeline

  1. 1987 – The garage opens. Walt repairs his first 40 bikes in a single summer.
  2. 1994 – Move to Main Street. The shop gets a real storefront and a second work stand.
  3. 2003 – First Sunday ride. Eleven riders, one flat tire, zero regrets.
  4. 2012 – Gravel and e-bikes arrive. The lineup doubles almost overnight.
  5. 2020 – Online booking. Test rides and tune-ups can be reserved from home.
  6. 2026 – Fall catalog. Four models, four group rides a week, and a new generation in the saddle.
