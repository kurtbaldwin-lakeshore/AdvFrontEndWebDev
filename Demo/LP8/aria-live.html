aria-live

polite waits its turn; assertive interrupts immediately — reserve assertive for genuine errors.

aria-live — Click to Announce

Click the button — the status message below updates. With a screen reader running, this update gets announced automatically, without moving keyboard focus there.

index.html 
<button id="save-btn">Save Changes</button>
<div aria-live="polite" id="live-status"></div>

<!-- polite: waits for a natural pause, doesn't interrupt -->
<!-- assertive: interrupts immediately, reserve for real errors -->
