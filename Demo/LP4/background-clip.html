background-clip

Clip a background to something other than the whole box — here, to the shape of the text itself.

index.html 
<h2 class="bc-gradient-text">Gradient Heading Text</h2>
style.css 
.bc-gradient-text {
  background: linear-gradient(90deg, #145a78, #a65314);
  -webkit-background-clip: text;
  background-clip: text;
  color: transparent;
}

/* Without color: transparent, the text color paints */
/* over the gradient and hides it completely */
