Bootstrap Forms

form-control, form-label, and the floating label pattern.

form-control and form-label — Standard Pattern

Click the label — focus jumps to the input, exactly like plain HTML labels. Bootstrap just adds consistent spacing and styling.

index.html 
<!-- Standard Bootstrap form field -->
<div class="mb-3">
  <label for="email" class="form-label">Email address</label>
  <input type="email" class="form-control" id="email">
</div>

Floating Labels — Label Animates on Focus

Click into the field — the placeholder label animates up and out of the way instead of disappearing.

index.html 
<!-- Floating label — label animates into the field on focus -->
<div class="form-floating mb-3">
  <input type="email" class="form-control" id="emailFloat"
         placeholder="name@example.com">
  <label for="emailFloat">Email address</label>
</div>
